1. Introduction

Welcome to WordCount Pro ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at wordcountpro.in. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

By using WordCount Pro, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited, non-personal data to operate and improve the website:

  • Usage Data: Browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring URLs — collected automatically via Google Analytics.
  • Cookie Data: Small data files placed in your browser for functionality and advertising purposes (see Section 5).
  • Contact Form Data: If you email us, we collect your email address and message content solely to respond to your inquiry.

We do NOT collect: The text you enter into the word counter tool. All text processing happens client-side (inside your browser). Your writing never leaves your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain the WordCount Pro website

  • To display relevant advertisements (Google AdSense)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as page views, session duration, and geographic location at a country level.
