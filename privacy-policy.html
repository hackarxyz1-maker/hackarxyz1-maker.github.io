1. Introduction

Welcome to WordCount Pro (https://wordcountpro.in). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our online word counting and text analysis tool.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

WordCount Pro is designed with privacy as our top priority. We do NOT collect or store any personal information such as:

  • Your name or email address
  • Phone number or contact information
  • User accounts or login credentials
  • Your location data
  • Device identifiers or IP addresses for tracking purposes
  • Cookies that track your behavior

3. Client-Side Processing (Your Privacy Protection)

🔒 Your text stays on YOUR device:

WordCount Pro operates entirely on your browser (client-side). This means:
  • All text you enter is processed locally in your web browser
  • Your text is NEVER sent to our servers
  • Your text is NEVER stored on external servers
  • You can use this tool completely offline
  • Your data is 100% private and secure

4. No Analytics or Tracking

WordCount Pro does not use:

  • Google Analytics or similar tracking services
  • Advertising networks or pixel tracking
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Behavioral analytics
  • Third-party data collection services

5. How We Use Information (Minimal)

We may collect basic, non-personal technical information to improve our website, such as:

  • Browser type and version (not linked to you)
  • Pages visited (aggregate statistics only)
  • General geographic region (country level, not specific location)

This information is anonymous and never used to identify you personally.

6. Third-Party Services

WordCount Pro may use minimal third-party services for website functionality:

  • Web Hosting: Our website is hosted on secure servers
  • Google AdSense (Optional): We may display non-personalized ads. These do not track your personal information

We do not share any data with third parties. Your text content is never transmitted externally.

7. Cookies

WordCount Pro may use minimal cookies for:

  • Website functionality and security
  • Remembering your preferences (optional)

These cookies do NOT track your behavior across the internet and do NOT contain personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

WordCount Pro is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since we do not collect personal data at all, children's privacy is inherently protected on our platform.

9. Data Security

Your security is important to us. Since all processing happens in your browser:

  • Your data is never transmitted over the internet (unless you copy-paste externally)
  • We use HTTPS encryption for secure website connection
  • No passwords or sensitive data are stored
  • Your device's built-in security protects your data

10. Links to Other Websites

WordCount Pro may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated whenever changes are made.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy.
