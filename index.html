Does it count spaces as characters?

We provide both metrics. The main "Characters" card includes spaces, while the detailed metrics section shows "Characters (no spaces)". This is useful for social media posts like X (Twitter) or SMS limits.

How is reading time calculated?

We use the industry standard of 200 words per minute (WPM) for reading time and 130 WPM for speaking time. This helps content creators estimate how long their script or blog post will take to consume.
