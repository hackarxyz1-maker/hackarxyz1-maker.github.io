About Us

Welcome to Word Counter Tool, a fast, free, and privacy-friendly online utility designed to help writers, students, bloggers, and professionals analyze their text efficiently.

What We Do

Our tool provides accurate word count, character count (with and without spaces), sentence count, paragraph count, reading time estimation, and more — all in real time.

Why Choose Us

We believe in simplicity, speed, and privacy. All text processing happens directly in your browser. We do not store, track, or transmit any user data.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide clean, distraction-free, and reliable writing tools that work instantly on any device without requiring signups or downloads.
