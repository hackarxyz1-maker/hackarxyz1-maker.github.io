About WordCount Pro

WordCount Pro is a free, fast, and privacy-first online text analysis tool built for writers, students, bloggers, SEO professionals, and anyone who works with words every day. Our mission is simple: give you instant, accurate, and comprehensive metrics about your writing — with zero compromise on your privacy.

We are an independent web tool, proudly hosted at wordcountpro.in. We are not affiliated with any word processor company or content platform.

Our Privacy Commitment

At WordCount Pro, privacy is not an afterthought — it is the foundation of how we built this tool. Unlike many online utilities that send your text to remote servers for processing, every word you type stays entirely within your browser.

We process everything using JavaScript that runs locally on your device. This makes our tool safe for:

  • Confidential business documents and reports
  • Private creative writing and personal journals
  • Academic papers and student assignments
  • Sensitive emails and professional correspondence

Who We Are

WordCount Pro was created by an independent developer passionate about building simple, useful web tools. Our goal is to provide a professional-grade writing utility that anyone in the world can access for free, without sign-ups, downloads, or privacy concerns.

We are based in India and serve users globally. Our commitment is to keep this tool free, fast, and continuously improved based on user feedback.
